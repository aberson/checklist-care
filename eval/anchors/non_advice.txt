Morning weather and traffic report for the Riverside district

Weather. The night was clear and cold, and the lowest temperature at the airport station was
two degrees. A band of high cloud arrived from the west shortly after sunrise. By nine o'clock
the temperature had reached seven degrees, and a light wind was blowing from the south-west at
about ten kilometres an hour. The forecast models agree that the cloud thickens through the
afternoon and that light rain reaches the coast in the early evening. Rainfall totals are
expected to stay under three millimetres. Tomorrow begins grey, with a high of nine degrees.
The river gauge at the old mill reads one point four metres, which is normal for the season.

Traffic. The ring road was busy between seven and half past eight, as it is on most weekdays.
A delivery van broke down on the northbound slip road near the market at a quarter to eight,
and one lane was closed for about forty minutes while it was towed away. Both lanes are open
again. Roadworks on Station Street continue this week; the street is one-way towards the
harbour until Friday. The number 12 bus is running about five minutes behind its timetable
because of the diversion. The ferry is on schedule. The level crossing at Mill Lane was closed
for six minutes at ten past eight for a freight train.

That is the report for this morning. The next report follows at noon.
